How we work

  1. Bounded before open-ended. The first piece of work has an end date and a deliverable. Retainers come after evidence, not instead of it.
  2. Written outcomes. What was found, what was changed and what is still wrong, in a document your team can act on without us in the room.
  3. Your repository, your infrastructure, your accounts. Nothing runs on our side and nothing needs us to keep running.
  4. We say no to work that will not help. If the problem is a hiring problem or a product decision, the audit will say so.
