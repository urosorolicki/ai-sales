Infrastructure that stops being the reason releases are slow.

Foldnine works with European product companies on the parts of the system nobody owns yet — the pipeline, the cluster, the service that pages somebody at night — and on the backend and frontend work around them.

What we do

Infrastructure audit

There is evidence of a problem and no clear cause. Two weeks, ending in a written report that names where the time goes and what to do first — useful whether or not you hire us for the rest.

Improvement sprint

One bounded problem you can already describe: a forty-minute build, a release that needs a manual step, a migration that stalled. Fixed scope, fixed end date.

Fractional DevOps and SRE

An ongoing need without a full-time hire to cover it. A set number of days a month, owning the platform work rather than advising on it.

Backend and frontend

The service, the API or the interface that the infrastructure work keeps running into. Built to the same standard as the platform under it, in your repository and your stack.

How we work

  • Bounded before open-ended. The first piece of work has an end date and a deliverable. Retainers come after evidence, not instead of it.
  • Written outcomes. What was found, what was changed and what is still wrong, in a document your team can act on without us in the room.
  • Your repository, your infrastructure, your accounts. Nothing runs on our side and nothing needs us to keep running.
  • We say no to work that will not help. If the problem is a hiring problem or a product decision, the audit will say so.

Who this is for

Product companies in Europe running their own infrastructure, roughly twenty to five hundred people, at the point where the platform work is real but a dedicated platform team is not yet. Usually Kubernetes, Terraform and a cloud bill that has started getting attention.
